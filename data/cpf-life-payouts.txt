CPF LIFE: Lifelong Retirement Payouts

CPF LIFE (Lifelong Income For the Elderly) is a national annuity scheme
administered by the Central Provident Fund Board that provides Singapore
Citizens and Permanent Residents with a monthly payout for as long as they
live, starting from their payout eligibility age. Members join CPF LIFE
automatically if they have at least S$60,000 in their Retirement Account
six months before their payout eligibility age.

The default payout eligibility age is 65, though members can choose to
start payouts as early as age 65 or defer to as late as age 70. Deferring
the start of payouts increases the monthly payout amount, by roughly 7%
for each year deferred, because the same retirement savings are spread
over a shorter expected payout period.

There are three CPF LIFE plans: the Standard Plan, the Basic Plan, and the
Escalating Plan. The Standard Plan pays a level monthly amount for life and
leaves a smaller bequest to beneficiaries. The Basic Plan pays a lower
monthly amount initially but leaves a larger bequest. The Escalating Plan
starts with a lower payout that increases by 2% each year, helping payouts
keep pace with inflation over a long retirement.

CPF LIFE payouts are funded from the Retirement Account, which itself is
formed from savings in the Ordinary Account and Special Account (or
Retirement Account, post-55) once a member reaches age 55, up to the
applicable Full Retirement Sum. Members who have less than the Basic
Retirement Sum can still join CPF LIFE with a lower starting premium, and
their payouts will be correspondingly smaller.

Because CPF LIFE is a pooled annuity, members who live longer than average
continue to receive payouts for as long as they live, cross-subsidized by
members who have a shorter payout period, which is the core insurance
function of the scheme.
